# University career-services guidance (verified sources)

Consulted **2026-10-08**. Paraphrased, not quoted. No page showed a publication or update date.
Re-check a page before citing a precise figure; pages change. These are recommendations,
not rules: the job posting and the target market override them.

## Harvard — Mignone Center for Career Success
URL: https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/hes-create-impactful-resumes-and-cover-letters/
Title: "HES: Create Impactful Resumes and Cover Letters"
- Résumé: action verbs; show results and impact; quantify when possible; factual claims.
- Order headings by relevance to the role; reverse chronological entries; trim education to what matters.
- Tailor each application; take keywords from the job description.
- Consistent formatting, white space; check layout after PDF conversion; same font in résumé and letter.
- Leave out photo, age, gender, references unless requested.
- Cover letter: role and fit → one or two specific examples → closing; brief, specific, **one page max**;
  address a named person when possible; plain, confident tone.
- ATS: mentioned only generally (write for human and ATS readers).
- **Not stated:** font names, font sizes, margins, a résumé page limit, columns.

## MIT — Career Advising & Professional Development
URLs: https://capd.mit.edu/channels/make-a-resume-cover-letter-cv/ (hub), https://capd.mit.edu/channels/resumes/
- Tailor documents to the job and team; proofread; use a checklist before sending.
- Résumé: strong action verbs, achievement-focused bullets; typical content: education, experience, skills, achievements.
- Cover letter: why this organization, why you fit, transferable skills with brief examples.
- CV conventions follow the country where you apply.
- ATS: only a linked resource title; no guidance on the pages read.
- **Not stated on pages read:** font names or sizes, margins, one-page rule, photos, quantification, ATS formatting.

## UC Berkeley — Career Engagement
URL: https://career.berkeley.edu/prepare-for-success/resumes/ (title "Resumes - Career Engagement")
- Results over duties; quantify when possible; action verbs; concise phrasing.
- Typical order: contact, education, skills, then experience / research / projects / volunteer sections.
- **One page** for college students and recent graduates; longer only with extensive relevant experience.
- **10–11 pt** font; **0.5–1 inch** margins.
- To stay ATS-friendly: avoid headers, footers, text boxes, tables, colors, pictures and graphics;
  conventional section titles; Word, Google Doc or PDF, following the posting's instructions.
- Map each posting's required and preferred qualifications to your accomplishments.
- **Not stated:** cover-letter guidance (separate section, not read). Only font named: Times New Roman (as an example).

## Yale — Office of Career Strategy
URL: https://ocs.yale.edu/channels/cover-letters-correspondence/
Title: "Cover Letters & Correspondence"
- **3–4 paragraphs, one page.** Opening and closing 1–3 sentences; body paragraphs 3–5 sentences.
- Opening: who you are, the role, why this employer, 2–3 related skills.
  Body: one skill per paragraph with examples, without restating the résumé.
  Closing: summarize, restate interest, invite a conversation.
- Address a specific person when possible (full name, title); otherwise "Dear Hiring Manager".
- Clear, readable font (Arial or Times New Roman given as examples); use keywords from the posting.
- **Not stated:** ATS, font sizes, margins, photos.
